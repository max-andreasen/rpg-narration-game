The Rusty Sword is a relic from the original village militia that defended the valley during the Age of Ruin. While its blade is pitted and dull, the iron was forged on Garrick’s anvil, ensuring it will not snap even under extreme pressure. It is often found discarded in the village outskirts, a reminder of the peace Valerius bought with his magic.

The Healing Potion is a distillation of sunlight and Ember-Wheat husks, bottled in a vial that keeps the liquid at a constant lukewarm temperature. The glowing red hue comes from crushed Cinder-Wood bark, which accelerates the body's natural recovery. It is a staple for those crossing the twelve basalt pillars into the wilds.

The Sturdy Rope is known as Talon-Grip cordage, a specialty item sold by Silas the Stout at the Crossroads. It is woven from reinforced Iron-Bark fibers and soaked in a mixture of resin and mountain goat fat to provide maximum friction against the Slick-Stone cliffs of the Rocky Hills.

The Compass is the discarded instrument of the navigator Aethelred, who left it at the Misty Lake when he realized he no longer cared where he was going. The needle is magnetized using subterranean lodestone, allowing it to bypass the spatial distortions of the Enchanted Forest and point toward a fixed geographical reality.

The Lantern is the Ferryman’s Beacon, an ancient tool recovered from the entrance hall of the Forgotten Crypt. It burns Ever-Glow Ichor, a bioluminescent fluid that emits a specific blue-tinted frequency capable of stabilizing moving shadows and repelling the photophobic Giant Spider of the caves.

The Bone Key is carved from the calcified rib of a traitor who once attempted to open the village gates to Malakor. It was later enchanted and hidden within the crypt to serve as the only physical bypass for the Seal of Seven Sorrows at the base of the Obsidian Tower.

The Mana Crystal is an Azure Resonator fragment harvested from the walls of the Crystal Cavern. These crystals act as natural capacitors for ley energy, humming with a frequency that can amplify the potency of nearby spells or be ground into Star-Silt for high-level alchemy.

The Diamond, or Earth-Eye, is formed by the immense pressure of the Rocky Hills pressing down upon the Crystal Cavern over millennia. Beyond its material value, the gem is said to be a "memory-anchor" that is immune to the dampening effects of the Misty Lake and the Obsidian Tower.

The Apple is a Valley-Sweet, a fruit grown in the orchards protected by the village's silver mist. Because the trees are nourished by the honey-tasting water of the central well, the fruit has minor restorative properties that can soothe the "Mana-Fever" caused by high-energy environments.

The Raw Fish is a Fog-Fin, a blind species native to the Misty Lake that navigates by sensing the memories trapped in the water. Its flesh is cold to the touch and tastes slightly of rain; it is often used by travelers to bait the Water Spirit or distract the Giant Spider.

The Glowing Mushroom is a Glow-Cap, the primary export of the Starting Village cultivated in secret cellar gardens. While it provides a reliable source of dim light, the fungal spores are mildly psychoactive, leading to the "Closet-Casting" visions reported by some villagers.

The Dark Crystal is a shard of Hate-Glass that has fallen from the exterior of the Obsidian Tower. It functions as a sensory void, absorbing nearby light and heat to fuel its internal cold. Merely holding the shard causes a spiritual drain, making it a dangerous but potent component for dark rituals.

The Ancient Scroll is a brittle page of Star-Slayed Silk salvaged from Malakor’s Chronos-Laboratory. It is written in the Cipher of the Unmaking, containing fragmented instructions on how to reverse the Great Stasis or unwrite the protective runes of the Starting Village.
