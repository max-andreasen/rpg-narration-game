Long before kingdoms claimed the mountains, the Emberborn dragons carved their homes in molten caverns beneath the world’s crust. These dragons were unlike the brutal wyrms of northern legend; their flames were not weapons but language. Each burst of fire conveyed emotion, memory, even prophecy.

According to old scrolls, humans once forged a pact with these dragons: a promise to guard the volcanic gateways in exchange for glimpses of the future. But humans broke the pact when they tried to trap an Emberborn egg, hoping its fire would grant immortality. The dragons vanished deep underground, taking their prophetic flames with them.

Some say the caverns still glow in rhythmic pulses — the dragons’ language echoing through stone, waiting for someone worthy to understand them again.
